BMW, short for Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, is a renowned German automobile manufacturer with a rich history dating back to 1916. Originally founded as an aircraft engine producer during World War I, BMW shifted its focus to motorcycles and automobiles in the 1920s. The company introduced its first car, the BMW 3/15, in 1929. Over the decades, BMW earned a reputation for engineering excellence, performance, and luxury.
